## Act 843 in brief

[The Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) applies to any person or business that processes personal data in Ghana, or that offers goods or services to data subjects in Ghana. The Data Protection Commission (DPC) is the regulator, and registration as a data controller is a statutory requirement.]

### The eight data protection principles

[Briefly, Act 843 requires that personal data be:
1. Processed fairly, lawfully, and with the data subject's consent where required.
2. Collected for specified, explicit, and legitimate purposes only.
3. Adequate, relevant, and not excessive.
4. Accurate and, where necessary, kept up to date.
5. Kept no longer than is necessary.
6. Processed in accordance with the rights of data subjects under Act 843.
7. Protected by appropriate technical and organisational security measures.
8. Not transferred to a country outside Ghana unless that country provides an adequate level of protection, or one of the statutory exceptions applies.]

### Registration with the DPC

[Data controllers must register and renew their registration annually. The application requires a compliance self-assessment, supporting documentation, and the applicable registration fee.]

### Common pitfalls

[We frequently see:
- No documented privacy notice for website visitors and customers.
- No register of processing activities (RPA).
- No data processing agreement (DPA) with third-party vendors who touch personal data.
- Lack of a data breach response plan.
