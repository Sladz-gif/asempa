## Title verification before you pay

[Purchasing property in Accra is a major commitment. A disciplined, staged approach to due diligence and verification is the single best way to avoid a costly dispute later. Below is the non-exhaustive checklist we walk through with every buyer client.]

### 1. Root of title

[Ask the seller to produce every document that forms the chain of title — customary grant, allocation letters, building permits, assignment deeds, and previous sale agreements. A gap of more than a few years should prompt further enquiry.]

### 2. Lands Commission searches

[We conduct formal searches at the Lands Commission (Title Registry and Deeds Registry, depending on the type of title) to verify what is officially recorded against the property and whether any caution or caveat has been lodged.]

### 3. Customary enquiries

[For many properties in Accra, stool or family land enquiries are essential. We work with experienced local counsel where needed to confirm the grantors' authority and confirm that no competing customary grant exists.]

### 4. Court and litigation search

[We search whether any party to the transaction is currently involved in litigation affecting the property, which can affect the seller's ability to pass good title.]

### 5. Physical inspection and survey

[A licensed surveyor should verify beacon positions and confirm that the land being sold matches the description in the documents before any agreement is signed.]

### 6. Agreement, completion, and post-completion

[Once due diligence is complete, we prepare or review the sale agreement, supervise completion, manage stamp duty, and file for registration of your interest.
