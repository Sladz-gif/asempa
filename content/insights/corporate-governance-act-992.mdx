## An operating model for Act 992

[Since the Companies Act, 2019 (Act 992) came fully into force, boards and company secretaries in Ghana have had to adapt their governance practices to a substantially more prescriptive regime. This article summarises the key changes and suggests a practical operating model for companies of all sizes.]

### Directors' duties — now codified

[Act 992 codifies directors' core duties — to act in the best interests of the company, exercise independent judgment, avoid conflicts of interest, and ensure the company can meet its debts as they fall due. These duties are owed to the company directly and are enforceable by members through derivative actions.]

### Composition of the board

[Public companies and companies above a size threshold must now have a minimum number of independent directors and, where applicable, an audit committee with specific composition requirements. Private companies have more flexibility but still benefit materially from documented board charters and clearly delegated authority.]

### Filings and the new electronic register

[The Registrar General's Department has steadily moved filings to electronic channels. We recommend that every company maintain a rolling compliance calendar covering annual returns, director and shareholding updates, and filings relating to charges over the company's assets.]

### Takeaways for boards

[1. Review and refresh your articles of association to align with Act 992.
2. Document board charters and committee terms of reference.
3. Maintain a clear calendar of statutory filings.
4. Schedule an annual governance health check with your external legal advisors.
